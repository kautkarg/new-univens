## Our Ideal Customer Profile

* **Primary ICP:** Business decision-makers (founders, CEOs/CXOs) at startups, SMEs and growing companies. These leaders need reliable technology/digital execution and specialist expertise to achieve priorities without expanding in-house teams.  
* **Secondary ICP:** Commercial and distribution partners (IT consultants, agencies, sales teams, or enterprise partners). They have client relationships or market access but need a dependable engineering/delivery partner to fulfill projects.  
* **Key Needs (Primary):** Proven technical capability; clarity on project ownership and outcomes; alignment with business objectives; trustworthy execution.  
* **Key Needs (Secondary):** A collaborative partner to meet their client’s tech requirements; flexible engagement models; consistent delivery quality.  
* **Decision Drivers (Both):** Demonstrated capability and experience; deep business understanding; clear accountability and communication; agility to adapt to changing objectives.

## Typography

* **Typefaces (UNSPECIFIED):** The primary font should be a modern sans-serif (e.g. *Humanité* or *Roboto*). A secondary complementary font (sans or serif) may be used for accents or quotes. *Exact fonts and weights to be confirmed from brand guidelines.*  
* **Weight & Style:** Use a bold/black weight for headings (H1, H2) and medium/regular for body text. Italics or semibold for highlights. Maintain consistency in heading levels (e.g. H1 \~36pt, H2 \~24pt, body \~14–18pt).  
* **Hierarchy:** Define a clear type scale (e.g., Title, Subtitle, Heading, Body, Caption) with consistent font sizes and line heights. For PPT: titles \~32–40pt, bullets \~18–22pt (UNSPECIFIED exact).  
* **Legibility:** Avoid script or decorative fonts. Keep letter-spacing and line-spacing open for readability. Check against ACM design standards (clean, professional). Verify all text styles (caps, lower-case) against the Univens brand specs – mark any unknown specs as **UNSPECIFIED**.

## Graphic Language

* **Style:** Minimalist and geometric. Use simple shapes, lines or blocks to add interest (e.g. thin accent lines, squares or grids), reflecting material design principles. Avoid ornate or organic shapes.  
* **Layout:** Follow a single-column or modular grid. Align elements to a baseline grid for consistency. Use ample whitespace to create an editorial, document-like feel.  
* **Data/Illustration:** If using charts or icons, prefer line-art or flat icons in the brand color palette. Emphasize straight lines and right angles (per “straight lines only” direction).  
* **Color/Application:** Graphics should use the established brand colors (charcoal, green, cream) – no more than 2–3 on a given slide.  
* **Do’s vs Don’ts:**  
  * *Do:* Use network/flow diagrams with straight connectors, underline key points with solid bars. Use full-height colored blocks behind text sparingly.  
  * *Don’t:* Use 3D effects, gradients or stock “clip-art” style illustrations. No overly decorative flourishes that conflict with a technical/enterprise tone.

### Photography Direction

* **Tone:** Authentic, documentary-style; **no staged stock**. Use realistic light and compositions that suggest story.  
* **Core Territories (A–H):** High priority to (A) Business Impact, (B) Founder Ambition, (C) Community/Connection, (D) Ideas in Action, (E) Commerce & Access, (F) Progress/Perspective, (G) Teamwork, (H) Abstract visual metaphors.  
* **Content:** Show people and their products in real environments (e.g. entrepreneurs serving customers, founders at work, mentor meet-ups, product prototypes, digital payments in shops, urban architecture conveying scale). This aligns with *Visa/Razorpay/HSBC*\-style imagery (serene yet dynamic).  
* **Image Mix:** Aim \~60–80 curated images. Prioritize business-action shots and candid portraits. For variety, a suggested mix could be roughly A:25%, B:15%, C:10%, D:10%, E:10%, F:10%, G:10%, H:10%.

### Accessibility & Contrast

* Use high color contrast (text vs background) for legibility (meets WCAG guidelines).  
* Avoid small/light text on busy images. If needed, overlay semi-transparent neutral panels behind text.  
* Ensure type size is not too small and line spacing is adequate. All design elements (icons, diagrams) should be distinguishable by color and shape.

### Do’s / Don’ts

* **Do:** Prioritize clear hierarchy and purposeful graphics. Use branding colors meaningfully (blue for headings/buttons, green accents). Choose photos that show real outcomes. Write concise, value-focused captions.  
* **Don’t:** Use generic tech stock (servers, code screens, anonymous meetings). Don’t overload slides with more than 2–3 colors. Avoid using color alone to convey info. Never alter the Univens logo.

## **Frequently asked questions**

### **What is Univens?**

Univens is a business capability and execution partner. We work with founders, CXOs and business leaders to understand their requirements, bring together the right expertise and take ownership of getting the work done.

### **What can Univens help my business with?**

We support business requirements across technology, digital operations and growth. This includes website and product development, digital marketing, automation, business technology and specialist engineering. The capabilities involved depend on what your business needs.

### **How is Univens different from a traditional service provider?**

We don't begin with a fixed list of services. We begin by understanding your business requirement, defining the work and identifying the capabilities needed to deliver it. We then coordinate the relevant expertise and take responsibility for execution.

### **Can Univens help businesses looking to work with India?**

Yes. We help businesses explore relevant technology and execution capabilities within India's growing business ecosystem. Depending on the requirement, this can involve specialist expertise, engineering support and suitable business or delivery partnerships.

### **Do you work with Indian businesses as well?**

Absolutely. We work with businesses in India that want to build, improve or expand their operations, as well as businesses exploring opportunities beyond their existing markets. The focus is always on the requirement, not the location.

### **Do I need to know exactly which service I need?**

No. You can approach us with a defined project, a specific requirement or a business problem you are trying to solve. We will work with you to understand the need, establish the scope and identify the right way to proceed.

### **Who will handle the execution?**

Univens takes responsibility for coordinating the engagement and its agreed deliverables. We bring in relevant specialists and delivery partners when required, while maintaining a clear point of contact and accountability.

### **How do we get started?**

Tell us what you are working on, what you need to achieve or where you need support. We will understand the requirement and discuss a practical approach, including the scope, capabilities and next steps.
